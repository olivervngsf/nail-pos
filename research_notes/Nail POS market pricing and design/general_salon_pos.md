# General salon/spa software and generic POS used by US nail salons (late 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-16. Method: web search only. Direct fetches of vendor pricing pages (squareup.com, fresha.com, zenoti.com) were blocked by this session's network, so almost all numbers come from third-party pricing trackers and competitor blogs (CostBench, SchedulingKit, Pabau, Koalendar, GlossGenius/StyleSeat/Zenoti/Mangomint-owned blogs). Treat every price as **"reported, not verified on vendor page"** unless marked otherwise. Many sources are competitor marketing; that is flagged where relevant.

---

## 1. Subscription pricing, add-ons, marketplace and payout fees

### Takeaway
Pricing splits into three models: per-staff/per-calendar (Fresha, Vagaro, Booksy), per-location flat tiers (Square, GlossGenius, Boulevard, Meevo, Booker), and quote-only enterprise (Zenoti, Clover salon). For a 10-tech nail salon, per-seat tools land at roughly $100–$200/mo, premium per-location tools at $140–$600/mo, plus marketplace commissions (Fresha 20%, Booksy Boost 30%) and metered texting. A flat, all-staff price is a real differentiator against Fresha, Vagaro and Booksy.

### Cited Findings

**Fresha**
- US Independent plan $19.95/mo for one bookable member; Team plan $14.95 per bookable team member/mo; Enterprise by quote for >20 team members; 7-day trial. 5-person team ≈ $74.75/mo, 10-person ≈ $149.50/mo — [GlossGenius blog (competitor)](https://glossgenius.com/blog/fresha-pricing); [Pabau (competitor, "verified April 10, 2026")](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-pricing/)
- Marketplace new-client fee: 20% commission per new client booked via the Fresha marketplace — [CostBench](https://costbench.com/software/salon-spa/fresha/hidden-costs/); Fresha's own help-center page exists on this topic: [Fresha help: Marketplace new client fees](https://fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/billing-and-fees/188-marketplace-new-client-fees) (not fetched)
- Metered messaging: free allowance of 100 texts and 50 marketing emails per team member/mo, then $0.02 each; "Insights" add-on $9.95 per bookable team member — [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-pricing/)
- Fresha removed its "free forever" plan in early 2025 (Booksy says March 2025); salon owners complained on Reddit (r/BehindTheChair) — [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-pricing/); [Booksy blog](https://biz.booksy.com/blog/best-salon-software-2026); [Salonist](https://salonist.io/blog/switch-from-fresha-to-salonist)
- Conflicting trackers: CostBench logged a Feb 2026 "price decrease" (top plan $60 → $19.95, later $12.95); StackScored still lists a $0 commission plan — treat as unreliable — [CostBench changelog](https://costbench.com/changelog/fresha-price-decrease-2026-02/); [StackScored](https://www.stackscored.com/pricing/salon-spa-management/fresha/)

**Clover**

**Nail-specific newcomers (rising)**

- Tilavon: walk-in turn management, commission tracking; English/Vietnamese/Spanish/Chinese (G2 listing) — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/tilavon/discuss)

### Inferences
- Example 10-tech salon monthly software (before processing): Fresha ≈ $150 + texts; Vagaro ≈ $114–$120 + add-ons; Booksy ≈ $210; Square Plus ≈ $49; GlossGenius Platinum ≈ $168; Boulevard ≈ $176–$410; Booker $139–$599. Our ~$39–49 flat price undercuts all but Square Plus.
- Fresha and Booksy's marketplace commissions (20–30% of a new client's first visit) are a recurring pain point; "no new-client fee" is an easy, credible message.
- Per-seat pricing punishes nail salons, which have many techs at low ticket values.

### Gaps
- Could not open any vendor pricing page directly (network block); all numbers need verification on fresha.com, vagaro.com, squareup.com, glossgenius.com, joinblvd.com, booksy.com.
- Square Appointments Free plan staff/location limits not confirmed.
- Payroll add-on prices (Vagaro, Square Payroll, Fresha) not found in this pass.
- Extra-location pricing not found for most vendors (most appear per-location).

## 2. Card processing rates and no-show/deposit fees

### Takeaway
In-person rates cluster at 2.2–2.7% + $0.10–0.20; online ~2.8–2.9% + $0.20–0.30; keyed 3.3–3.5%. GlossGenius' flat 2.6% (no per-ticket fee) moved to tiered rates in Oct 2026. No-show/deposit charges are usually just normal processing on a saved card, not a separate fee.

### Cited Findings
- **Fresha (US):** in-person 2.29% + $0.20; online 2.79% + $0.20; manual entry 3.30% + $0.20; Tap to Pay +$0.10/authorization. No separate no-show fee — deposits/no-show charges on saved cards pay standard processing; a charged no-show fee cannot be reversed — [SchedulingKit](https://schedulingkit.com/pricing-guides/fresha-pricing); [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-review/); [Fresha help: no-show fees](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/payments/617-charge-no-show-and-cancellation-fees)
- **Square:** in-person 2.6% + 15¢ (Free), 2.5% + 15¢ (Plus), 2.4% + 15¢ (Premium); keyed/card-on-file 3.5% + 15¢. Square's own POS pricing page reportedly shows 2.6% + 10¢ (conflict) — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/small-business/square-fees); [Square POS pricing](https://square.com/us/en/point-of-sale/software/pricing)
- **Square Appointments online:** 3.3% + 30¢ on Free, 2.9% + 30¢ on paid — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/170263/Square-Appointments/pricing/)
- **Vagaro:** Small-merchant 2.6% + $0.10 card-present (under $4k/mo); larger merchants 2.2% + $0.19; another source says 2.75% flat — [GlossGenius blog](https://glossgenius.com/blog/vagaro-pricing-a-complete-breakdown-of-costs); [SchedulingKit](https://www.schedulingkit.com/pricing-guides/vagaro-pricing)
- **GlossGenius:** flat 2.6% across payment types, no per-ticket fee — [GlossGenius vs StyleSeat](https://glossgenius.com/blog/glossgenius-vs-styleseat); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/174830/GlossGenius/pricing/). Changed to tiered rates in Oct 2026 — [GlossGenius price change tracker](https://getpulsesignal.com/changes/glossgenius) (new tiers not found)
- **Booksy:** 2.69% + $0.30 for manual mobile entry (single aggregator) — [Koalendar](https://koalendar.com/blog/booksy-pricing)

### Inferences
- A nail salon doing $30k/mo in card sales pays ~$700–$900/mo in processing on any of these, which dwarfs software fees. Being upfront about Stripe's fee per ticket (our "nothing taken silently" rule) is a real contrast with blended or pass-through fees (e.g., Vagaro's Mastercard fees).
- Stripe's standard online rate is in the same range as competitors' online rates, so QR/pay-link checkout won't be cheaper than card-present on Square/Fresha; we compete on clarity, not rate.

### Gaps
- Boulevard, Booker, Zenoti, Meevo, Clover processing rates not found (mostly quote-based).
- GlossGenius' new tiered rates (Oct 2026) not found.

## 3. Walk-in turn lists, commission/tip splits, check/cash payout, Vietnamese UI — and why nail salons leave or avoid them

### Takeaway
The mainstream platforms are built for appointment-based hair/spa; none of the big names were found to offer a true nail-style turn list, check/cash payout split, or Vietnamese staff UI. Those gaps are exactly why Vietnamese walk-in salons use niche nail POS (Zota, TLNA, Tilavon) or paper.

### Cited Findings
- Square tracks tips and staff hours, but "leaves the splitting logic to you" and has no built-in turn queue — [Zenoti blog (competitor)](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems); [Sonary](https://sonary.com/pos/salons)
- Zenoti offers a walk-in queue and technician turn management with auto tips per tech; argues nail POS must track whose turn it is, services done, and client wait time — [Zenoti nail salon POS](https://www.zenoti.com/salon-management-software/nail-salon-pos)
- GlossGenius is a poor fit for multi-tech nail shops: designed for solos/small teams, no turn management or multi-tech commission tracking — [Zenoti blog (competitor)](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems); GlossGenius described as "built with solo beauty professionals in mind" — [The Salon Business](https://thesalonbusiness.com/glossgenius-alternatives/)
- Vagaro rated strongest of the general tools for tip reports and payroll — [Zenoti comparison](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/nail-salon-software-comparison)
- Zota POS: Zenoti credits it with walk-in check-in and turns but calls it "a register with a check-in screen, not a business platform" — [Zenoti comparison](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/nail-salon-software-comparison)
- Vietnamese UI: Fresha's customer app lists Vietnamese among ~30 languages; its 2023 rollout (9 business languages) didn't name Vietnamese; business-side Vietnamese not confirmed — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/lc/app/fresha/id1297230801); [Newswire 2023](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/fresha-accelerates-global-growth-and-expands-into-new-markets-with-launch-of-platform-in-nine-languages-823260855.html). Fresha has hired Vietnamese-speaking onboarding staff (job post) — [Jobera](https://jobera.com/job/fresha-partner-onboarding-specialist-manila-vietnamese-speaking-a403caa8/)
- Niche tools with Vietnamese: TLNA (training in Vietnamese), Tilavon (UI in Vietnamese), Zota (bilingual) — [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/TLNA-POS/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/tilavon/discuss); [Zenoti blog](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems)
- Why owners leave: Fresha — removal of free plan, new-client commission, processing rates — [Pabau Fresha reviews](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-reviews/); Boulevard — 12-month contract, hard-to-find KPIs, slow location switching, payment inflexibility — [The Salon Business](https://thesalonbusiness.com/glossgenius-alternatives/); [Capterra review](https://www.capterra.com/p/180087/Boulevard/reviews/3344343/); [Portrait Care](https://www.portraitcare.com/post/boulevard-vs-glossgenius); Booker — auto-renew contracts and hidden implementation/training fees — [CostBench](https://costbench.com/software/salon-spa/booker-mindbody/); competitor claim that Mindbody has stopped developing Booker and is migrating users — [Zenoti vs Mindbody (competitor)](https://www.zenoti.com/en-uk/thecheckin/zenoti-vs-mindbody)

### Inferences
- No mainstream platform found does check/cash payout splits; this looks like open ground (combined with fair-pay transparency).
- Mainstream "Vietnamese support" is at best client-facing; staff UI in Vietnamese plus a customer English toggle is a differentiator vs. all big names, but not vs. niche nail POS (Zota/TLNA/Tilavon) — those are the direct competitors on this axis, and they appear to lack modern design and offline-first claims (unverified).

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads from Vietnamese nail owners surfaced; owner voice is thin. Interviews in the pilot should fill this.
- Could not confirm whether Fresha, Vagaro or Square offer any walk-in turn list (none was found in results; absence not proven).
- Commission rules (discounted vs full price, product vs service) per vendor not compared.

## 4. Design/UX reputation

### Takeaway
GlossGenius is widely seen as the cleanest, most design-forward, mobile-first tool (aimed at solos); Boulevard is the premium, polished choice for larger appointment salons/spas but carries contracts and complexity. Neither is built for multi-tech walk-in nail salons.

### Cited Findings
- GlossGenius: "clean, design-forward platform built with solo beauty professionals in mind"; mobile-focused, most functions in the app, fewer features than Boulevard — [The Salon Business (Mangomint-owned)](https://thesalonbusiness.com/glossgenius-alternatives/); [GlossGenius vs Boulevard](https://thesalonbusiness.com/glossgenius-vs-boulevard.md)
- Capterra ratings: GlossGenius 4.8 (348 reviews), ease of use 4.8; Boulevard 4.6 (365 reviews; 334 positive, 22 neutral, 9 negative) — [Capterra compare](https://www.capterra.com/compare/174830-180087/GlossGenius-vs-Boulevard)
- Boulevard criticism: reporting/KPIs hard to find; needs more custom reports, integrations, payment flexibility — [Capterra review](https://www.capterra.com/p/180087/Boulevard/reviews/3344343/); [Portrait Care](https://www.portraitcare.com/post/boulevard-vs-glossgenius)

### Inferences
- "Apple-like" in this category means GlossGenius-style: few screens, mobile-first, calm visuals, flat pricing. Our design target can borrow GlossGenius' polish while adding multi-tech depth (turns, payouts) that GlossGenius lacks.

### Gaps
- No neutral design review (e.g., UX teardown) found; reputation evidence is mostly from review scores and competitor blogs.
- Fresha/Vagaro/Square UX perception not gathered in this pass.

## 5. Notable 2024–2026 changes (price, fees, lawsuits, acquisitions)

### Takeaway
The biggest shift is Fresha ending free software (early/March 2025) in favor of per-seat subscriptions plus marketplace commission. Booker plans were renamed in Feb 2026 with Booker's long-term future questioned. GlossGenius changed processing to tiered rates (Oct 2026). No lawsuits/FTC actions were found against the salon platforms; the nearest regulatory signal is the FTC + 8 states suing Toast (restaurant POS) over undisclosed fees.

### Cited Findings
- Fresha removed free-forever plan early 2025 — [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-pricing/); March 2025 per [Booksy blog](https://biz.booksy.com/blog/best-salon-software-2026)
- Fresha acquired Australian marketplace Bookwell (date not captured) — [Salon Today](https://www.salontoday.com/articles/fresha-acquires-australian-platform-bookwell)
- Fresha added Klarna with a 5.99% US fee — [DesignRush News](https://news.designrush.com/fresha-adds-klarna-installment-payments)
- Booker plans renamed Feb 16, 2026 — [CostBench](https://costbench.com/changelog/booker-mindbody-plans-renamed-2026-02/); Mindbody bought Booker in 2018 for ~$150M (pre-2024 context) — [Salon Today](https://www.salontoday.com/articles/mindbody-to-acquire-booker-software)
- Zenoti acquired SuperSalon (date not captured) — [Salon Today](https://www.salontoday.com/articles/zenoti-acquires-supersalon-consolidating-leadership-in-salon-and-spa-software)
- DaySmart acquired UK salon platform Slick — [LLR Partners](https://www.llrpartners.com/daysmart-acquires-uk-salon-platform-slick-extending-its-global-reach-in-beauty-and-wellness/)
- GlossGenius: Apr 2026 Charting add-on replaced free medspa beta; Oct 2026 flat 2.6% → tiered processing; Nov 2026 possible new "Practice" plans ($168/$298) — [PulseSignal](https://getpulsesignal.com/changes/glossgenius); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/glossgenius)
- Vagaro intro pricing ($23.99 "first 6 months") vs $30 list — [Booknetic](https://www.booknetic.com/blog/vagaro-alternatives-wordpress)
- FTC and 8 states sued Toast over hidden hardware/software fees (restaurant POS, not salon) — [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/why-the-ftc-and-8-states-are-suing-payments-fintech-toast)
- No lawsuit/FTC action against Booksy found — searched; [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/unwrapping-the-money-behind-beautys-booking-platforms) covers its fee model only

### Inferences
- Regulators are watching hidden POS fees (Toast case); our "every ticket shows tip + card fee, nothing taken silently" principle is both a marketing and a compliance advantage.
- Market is consolidating (Zenoti, DaySmart, Fresha acquisitions), leaving room for a focused, nail-only, Vietnamese-first product.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 price-hike announcements confirmed for Square, Vagaro, Mindbody, Boulevard.
- No dates/prices for Bookwell, SuperSalon, Slick deals captured.
- Court-docket/FTC search for Fresha, Vagaro, Square-Appointments-specific actions not done.
