## Which nail POS brands serve Vietnamese-run salons, and do they exist?

### Takeaway
Confirmed as real and current in 2026: **Zota (ZotaSalon / Zota POS)**, **Mango POS (Mango for Salon)**, **Fastboy Marketing (Go POS + Go Check In)**, **NailSoft** and **POSNailStore**. Also seen: **TLNA POS**, **Galaxy Access**, **Tilavon**, **Astra POS** and the iPad app **iNailPOS**. Search found nothing on iPOS (as a nail POS), Lexi, NailSalon365, Techno/"Nails POS", Tima POS, Reach Nails or Salon Pro, so none of those are confirmed.

- Several new web-first nail POS brands appeared in 2026 (Tilavon, Astra, Galaxy Access, TLNA), so new entrants exist. Our product is not the only one with a Vietnamese/English pitch.

## Pricing, processing rates, contracts and cash discount

### Takeaway
The nail-specific vendors mostly hide their pricing. Reported figures: Fastboy Go POS from about $50/mo, Mango about $49–299/mo plus a $149–249 setup fee and a 3-month minimum, NailSoft from $99.95/mo. Zota is quote-only and bundled with processing. Card rates are typically about 2.2–2.9% in person. Cash discount programs and early termination fees of roughly $200–1,000 are common in this market.

### Cited Findings
- **Fastboy Go POS**: Capterra lists $50/mo for the Basic plan, up to 5 staff. A third-party site says $50–99/mo. Go Check In is listed at $59–99/mo, with add-ons priced separately. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10040943/Go-Checkin-POS/); [Capterra AU](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1095600/Go-Checkin-POS)
- **Fastboy Basic tier**: includes payment integration, **cash discount** and shortcuts/rewards. It does **not** include payroll, timekeeping or the POS calendar, so those cost more. It works with Bamboo Pay terminals and has QR-code checkout. — [Fastboy Go POS](https://en.fastboy.com/go-pos/)
- **Mango POS**: $49–299/mo, a $149–249 one-time setup fee and a 3-month minimum. Processing rates are not published. These figures come from Tilavon, a competitor, and are unverified. — [Tilavon vs Mango](https://tilavon.com/compare/mango-pos)
- **Zota**: no public pricing. It is bundled with hardware and processing, and sales gives quotes. — [Zenoti guide](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems); [twizzlo](https://twizzlo.com/articles/nail-salon-pos/)
- **NailSoft**: from $99.95/mo, Premium/Unlimited $149.95–249.95 (unit unclear). This comes from a single secondary source. — [Zenoti comparison](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/nail-salon-software-comparison)
- **Astra POS**: Free up to about $161/mo, Pro about $49.50/mo. It claims a 1.80% flat rate for eligible nail salons. This is the vendor's own page. — [astraposonline.com](https://astraposonline.com/best-pos-system-for-nail-salons.html)
- **Benchmarks from general POS tools**:
  - Square: Free, Plus $49 and Premium $149 per location; no contract or termination fee.
  - Clover: about $50/mo Starter, or hardware from about $599 plus $14.95/mo. Hardware subscriptions are non-cancelable, and terms vary by reseller.
  - SpotOn: $25/mo plus $200 hardware.
  - Vagaro: about $23.99/mo.
  - Mangomint: was $165/$245/$375 per month, with pricing changed Aug 1, 2026; payroll add-on $50/mo plus $8 per worker.
  - Sources: [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/best/salon-pos-software); [avada.io](https://avada.io/blog/pos-systems-for-nail-salon/); [SpotOn](https://www.spoton.com/small-business/nail-salon/); [Vagaro](https://www.vagaro.com/pro/nail-salon-software); [Mangomint blog](https://www.mangomint.com/blog/pricing-update-2026.md); [Mangomint payroll](https://www.mangomint.com/blog/introducing-payroll-processing.md)
- **Typical card rate**: 2.2–2.9% in person. Software usually runs $0 to about $200/mo per location. At $12k/mo in card sales, a 2.3% rate costs $3,312/yr and a 3.5% rate costs $5,040/yr. — [twizzlo](https://twizzlo.com/articles/nail-salon-pos/); [mycutsapp](https://www.mycutsapp.com/blog/best-salon-pos-systems)
- **Hardware**: Square reader $49, Square Terminal $299, iPad POS setups $300–600. — [twizzlo](https://twizzlo.com/articles/nail-salon-pos/)
- **Early termination fees** in nail-salon processing contracts are about $200–500 in one guide and up to $500–1,000 in another. Both guides are vendor-affiliated. — [sleftpayments](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/payment-processing-for-nail-salons-2026); [astraposonline](https://astraposonline.com/best-pos-system-for-nail-salons.html)
- **Cash discount programs**: these raise the sale total by about 3%, then take 3% off for cash payers. Mangomint argues this is effectively a surcharge and works badly for salons. A merchant-services page recommends it, as long as tips are left out of the surcharge. — [Mangomint "Is cash discounting a scam"](https://mangomint.com/blog/is-cash-discounting-a-scam); [paymullet](https://paymullet.vercel.app/industries/nail-salons.html)

### Inferences
- On a typical salon, processing costs far more than the software fee. A flat software price plus plain Stripe rates (about 2.7% + 5¢ for Terminal, 2.9% + 30¢ online) may cost *more* than a negotiated 1.8–2.3% rate, unless we pass card fees through openly. We should model this before setting the price.
- The key contract risks to beat are setup fees, 3-month or longer minimums, non-cancelable hardware leases and early termination fees. Our free 30-day pilot, no contract, and owner-owned iPad/phone counter these directly.

### Gaps
- Mango and Zota rates, Zota hardware bundle prices, and contract lengths for any nail-specific vendor: none were published.
- Whether Zota or Mango charge per-tech fees.

## Features: turn list, payroll, tips, check/cash split, Vietnamese, booking, texting

### Takeaway
Turn tracking and commission/payroll are standard in nail-specific tools: Mango, Zota and Fastboy all have them, with Fastboy charging more for payroll. Marketing by text plus check-in is Fastboy's core product. None of the sources mention a **check/cash payout split**. Vietnamese UI is claimed by the new entrants. For Mango it is disputed.

### Cited Findings
- **Mango**: has an automated turn system ("fairness and transparency... reducing the risk of favoritism"), plus commission management, reports, booking, and check-in/out. — [Mango POS](https://mangoforsalon.com/mango-pos/)
- **Mango and Vietnamese**: Tilavon says Mango has no Vietnamese, but the same page contradicts itself. This is unresolved. — [Tilavon](https://tilavon.com/compare/mango-pos)
- **Zota**: lists integrated card payments, "payroll calculations in minutes", check-in/out and turn management. Reviewers call it "a register with a check-in screen, not a business platform." — [zota.us/pos](https://zota.us/pos/); [Zenoti](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems)
- **Galaxy Access's comparison** lists Zota and Galaxy Access as having both turn tracking and Vietnamese support. — [galaxyaccess.us](https://galaxyaccess.us/best-pos-system-for-nail-salon/)
- **Fastboy Go POS**: POS, appointments, online booking, client management and inventory. Go Check In collects name, phone, email, birthday and visits, with automated SMS marketing and review requests. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10040943/Go-Checkin-POS/); [Go Check In](https://fastboy.com/services/go-checkin)
- **Go Checkin POS** is said to keep working during internet outages (from a Capterra snippet). — [Capterra AU](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1095600/Go-Checkin-POS)
- **Tip splitting and payroll calculation** are pitched as key nail-POS features by payment resellers. — [richpaymentsolutions](https://richpaymentsolutions.com/point-of-sale-system-for-nail-salon-tip-payroll/)

### Inferences
- Offline support is already claimed by at least Fastboy, so it is expected rather than unique. We should still prove ours in demos.
- The check/cash payout split, plus a tech-facing "print my own earnings by PIN", look like open ground. No source mentions either.
- Fastboy's split between Basic (with cash discount) and payroll as an upsell shows the industry habit of selling the core tech-pay feature at a higher tier. Our "all staff included, one price" pitch speaks directly to that.

### Gaps
- Detailed tip handling (card fee on tips, pooled vs per-tech), texting costs and 10DLC handling, and the real quality of Vietnamese UI for each vendor.

## Complaints from owners and techs

### Takeaway
No direct reviews of Zota, Mango, Fastboy or NailSoft were found on Reddit, BBB, Trustpilot or in Facebook groups. The complaint themes we did find come from adjacent POS vendors and buying guides: surprise fee increases, auto-renewing contracts with termination fees, processor lock-in, and data you can't take with you.

### Cited Findings
- Revel's Trustpilot reviews describe monthly fees raised without notice, a termination fee justified by an auto-renewal clause the reviewer said they never agreed to, and annual platform fees. — [Trustpilot Revel](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/revelsystems.com)
- Buyer checklists consistently advise checking:
  - term length and cancellation cost
  - whether the hardware only works with one processor
  - whether you can export your client data
  - Sources: [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/best/salon-pos-software); [twizzlo](https://twizzlo.com/articles/nail-salon-pos/)
- Zota is criticised as narrow: a register rather than a full platform. — [Zenoti](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems)
- The Fastboy App Store app ("FastBoy Makerting", misspelled) has no reviews. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1014908193)

### Inferences
- Trust, clear fees and freedom to leave (export your data, no lock-in) are likely selling points against the payments-led nail vendors. This needs confirming in pilot interviews.

### Gaps
- Real owner and tech reviews of Zota, Mango, Fastboy and NailSoft. To fill this, allow capterra.com, google.com/maps, reddit.com, bbb.org and trustpilot.com in the network settings, or ask pilot salons directly.

## How they sell

### Takeaway
They sell mainly through merchant-services resellers and payments companies that bundle the POS with card processing. Fastboy also sells a marketing package (check-in, SMS, reviews). We found no evidence about trade shows, nail supply stores or Vietnamese-speaking reps.

### Cited Findings
- Fastboy is resold by On Fire Merchant Services. — [On Fire](https://www.onfiremerchantservices.com/fastboy-go-check-in/)
- Zota's app is published by a payments company. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.payfirstsolutions.pos&hl=en_US)
- Many nail POS pages are written by payment processors (Rich Payment Solutions, Sleft Payments, Limelight Payments). — [richpaymentsolutions](https://richpaymentsolutions.com/point-of-sale-system-for-nail-salon-tip-payroll/); [sleftpayments](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/payment-processing-for-nail-salons-2026); [limelightpayments](https://www.limelightpayments.com/blog/top-7-salon-and-spa-pos-systems-for-2026)

### Inferences
- The incumbents' business is processing margin. A tool where the salon picks its own processor (Stripe) and sees every fee is a real point of difference, but owners may be getting low quoted rates or "free" cash discount setups. Elang should ask pilot salons what rate they pay and whether they run a cash discount program.

### Gaps
- Use of Vietnamese-speaking sales reps, presence at nail trade shows (e.g. Nail Expo, the Vietnamese nail community shows) and sales through nail supply stores. Nothing was found.

## Gaps a new product could exploit (summary of inferences)

### Takeaway
The likely openings are: clear and fair tech pay (check/cash split, techs can see their own earnings), no contract or setup fee, flat all-staff pricing with payroll included, the salon owns its hardware and data, and a strong Vietnamese UI. The main risk is that card processing, not software, drives the owner's total cost.

### Cited Findings
- Payroll is upsold above Fastboy's Basic tier. — [Fastboy Go POS](https://en.fastboy.com/go-pos/)
- Mango reportedly has a setup fee and a 3-month minimum. — [Tilavon](https://tilavon.com/compare/mango-pos)
- Zota is quote-only. — [twizzlo](https://twizzlo.com/articles/nail-salon-pos/)
- Cash discount programs are disputed. — [Mangomint](https://mangomint.com/blog/is-cash-discounting-a-scam)

### Inferences
- Competing new web-first entrants (Tilavon, Astra, Galaxy Access, TLNA) already pitch Vietnamese and low prices. Being different has to come from design quality, tech-pay transparency and reliability, not language alone.

### Gaps
- All of the above needs checking against real vendor pages and owner interviews. This session's network blocked direct page reads.
