## What would flip a no

- Another salon owner I know, Vietnamese like me, who has used it for months and tells me it never lost a ticket when the internet went down. Or a free trial where they set it up next to Square so I can try it for a few Saturdays without risk.
- A salon like mine with a similar size and staff already running it for months that I can visit or call, plus a run-alongside trial where I keep my current POS until my techs are comfortable.
- My sister-in-law or another owner I know using it for a few months and showing me their real card statement with lower fees, plus them moving my client list over for free.
- Another salon owner I know personally, in my area, who has used it for months and shows me it works. I'd also want a way to export all my data any time.
- Reserve with Google booking working, plus two or three Vietnamese salon owners from my Facebook group telling me they run it and their techs trust the payout sheet.
- Seeing a salon owner I know in my Facebook group using it for a few months, a no-internet Saturday demo at my own salon, and a promise they move my client list for free so I can run it next to my old POS for a month.
- Two or three big salons I know, with 11 or more techs, running it for over a year, plus a written promise that I can export all my data any time.
- Another salon owner I know and trust, running it for months, shows me it keeps taking cards when the Wi-Fi is down, and they let me try it free next to Square for a month without switching.
- Seeing two or three other Vietnamese salon owners in my Facebook group using it for a few months and saying it works offline on busy days, plus a free trial on my own iPad before I buy the $299 reader.
- They cover my processor exit fee, or my contract runs out. Then I'd want to see a salon like mine already using it, with the techs doing fine on it.
- A salon owner I know in the Facebook group tells me they switched and saved real money, and someone shows me my own statement against your 2.7% + 5 cents, including the exit fee, so I can see I come out ahead.
- My sister-in-law or another salon owner I know tells me they use it and their card fees went down.
