# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The small market to own first is clear: Vietnamese-run walk-in nail salons, starting in California (~80% Vietnamese-run). Small, close-knit, word of mouth. That is the right shape for this lens.
- A real secret, if it holds up: this is a payments market dressed as software. Rivals live on card fees, leases and lock-in. A flat fee with no markup is something they can't easily copy without cutting their own income.
- Something rivals don't seem to offer: tip + card fee on every ticket, a split between techs, a check/cash payout split, and techs printing their own pay by PIN. If techs come to trust only this paper, that is the start of a brand.
- The founders are insiders (Vietnamese salon people), so they can reach buyers through Facebook groups, Zalo and supply stores. Distribution starts from who they know, not from ads.
- The product is mostly built and works offline. The cost to serve is low (~$22–40 a salon a month).

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No moat, a crowded field.** ABC, Zota, Mango, Fastboy, Tilavon, TLNA, NailSoft and Square already sell to the same owner. New entrants already pitch "Vietnamese + low price". Cheaper is not 10x better. A rival can drop its price or add a payout sheet in six months.
2. **No proof of distribution.** 0 paying salons, 0 pilots signed, 1–2 interviews. One salesperson doing setup by hand is not a channel that reaches 100 salons. What each salon costs to win is unknown.
3. **Giving away the profit pool.** With no card markup, the business earns $39–49 a salon. The founders' own numbers say card fees are 10–20x that. Rivals can use card income to pay for free software, hardware and sales. That is a scale fight this business can't win.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is 10x better, not just cheaper? Can you show a tech or owner choosing you for it?
- What stops ABC or Zota adding a payout sheet and a $0 plan in month six?
- How do the first 100 salons arrive, through whom, and what does each one cost?
- What share of California Vietnamese walk-in salons can you own in 2 years: 5%? 20%?
- Do techs carry the brand from salon to salon? Could techs pull owners in (a network effect)?
- What locks a salon in after year 1 if you sell "no traps"? Is it data, payout history, the tip records?
- How do you earn enough to pay two founders at $49 with no card income?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- 3 pilot salons signed and live, with what they pay now and why they would switch.
- 10 paying salons in one city or network within 90 days, with the cost to win each one written down.
- Evidence techs push for it (they ask owners for it, or bring it to a new salon).
- A plan for what holds the position in year 3: tech network, tip and payout records, or a fair card margin.
