# Product lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- There is a clear "one thing" buried in it: **pay techs can trust.** Tip and card fee on every ticket, multi-tech tip split, check/cash payout split, techs print their own earnings by PIN. That answers the deepest pain in the research (techs can't check their pay, fees come out of card tips).
- It starts from the user. Senior, low-tech staff, phone number and PIN, big buttons, Vietnamese/English, works offline. That is starting from the experience, not the tech.
- Care in the parts nobody sees: integer-cent money, every change logged, closed days stay closed, honest reviews (no review gating). That kind of craft usually shows up where customers do look too.
- No traps is a product choice, not just a price: salon owns the reader and the data, no contract, export any time.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No focus.** V1 is checkout, payouts, turns, booking, calendar, gift cards, loyalty, promo texts, owner numbers, admin, a handheld app, several layout choices. Two people, 0 salons. Too many surfaces to make any one of them brilliant, and every extra screen is something a senior tech has to learn.
2. **Built before anyone used it.** Pain research is 1–2 people plus online reviews. Not one real salon has run a real Saturday on it. The product may be polished around guesses.
3. **Seams the salon will feel on day one.** Live database, live Stripe and texting are not switched on. Texting waits weeks on carrier paperwork. Printing on some handhelds needs a third-party app. A missing ZIP stops the reader. Each hand-off between systems is where a busy Saturday breaks, and the first bad Saturday ends the trial.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Say it in one sentence. Is it "the POS where techs trust their pay"? If yes, what would you cut to prove it?
- Walk me through the first five minutes for the owner, and the first busy Saturday for a tech. Where do they get stuck?
- Which features would you hide from pilot salons until they ask?
- How many salons have you watched check out a client and do payroll today, in person?
- What happens at the counter when the internet, the reader or the printer fails mid-ticket?
- Can a salon really be live after a 1-hour setup? Has anyone timed it?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Pick the one thing (trusted checkout + tech pay) and switch everything else off for the pilot.
- Turn on live database + live Stripe and run 2–3 real salons for 30 days; watch them in person at least one busy day each.
- Fix every seam found on setup day (ZIP, reader, printer) before salon #4.
- Come back with numbers that do not exist yet: setup time, tickets per day, tech complaints about pay, and whether owners stay after the free month.
