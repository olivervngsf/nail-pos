Gift cards + loyalty — how it works

Short answer. A gift card is only a key: the money lives in our database, and a card works only after the salon sells it at checkout. Points have no code: the client's phone number is the loyalty card. The owner sets it up once; the front desk runs it at checkout; nobody but the owner can change a balance by hand, and every change is logged.

Owner setup (once, ~15 min)

  1. Turn on Gift cards and Loyalty (owner PIN).
  2. Amounts $25/$50/$100/custom, $10–$500, never expire.
  3. Plastic cards: scan the first + last card of a box → In stock, $0.
  4. Digital card design (name, logo).
  5. Points rules: 1 per $1 of service, 100 pts = $10.
  6. Perks, packages, referrals (optional).
  7. Messages: receipt + email on; reward-ready text on.
  8. Permissions for manager and front desk.
  9. Test: sell a $1 card, use it, refund it.

Gift card codes

  • Where codes come from: always the system. The owner never types or invents one. Digital codes are made at the sale. Plastic: Gift cards · All codes · Make codes for printing → the system makes 200 random numbers → download the file for the card printer (they are added as In stock, $0). Cards that came already numbered are scanned in instead.
  • Print 3 ways: card printer file · code + QR stickers for blank cards · paper card on the receipt printer.
  • Add money (reload): at checkout, “Add money”, scan or find by phone → same code, new balance (max $500). Works for digital too.
  • Reuse a plastic card: a used-up ($0) plastic card can go back to stock (owner PIN): unlinked from the old client, history kept.
  • The owner sees every code in Gift cards · All codes: In stock (not sold) · Active (sold, money left) · Used up · Locked / Replaced · Voided, with balance, who sold it, to whom, last used.
  • Digital: made at the sale, 16 characters, random, e.g. MAIN-7K3Q-9XPA-2T4F. No 0/O/1/I; last character catches typos.
  • Plastic: the printed number + barcode; only numbers scanned into this salon's stock are accepted.
  • Stored hashed like a password; screens show only •••• 2T4F.
  • 5 wrong codes on one device = 15 min pause. Every use needs a staff PIN and is logged.

A card's life

In stock · $0→ sold + paid →Active · has a balance→ used to $0 →Used up
Active→ reported lost →Locked→ owner replaces →Replaced · balance on a new card · sale voided same day →Voided

Sell · take · check · lost

  1. Sell: + Gift card → amount → scan plastic or type receiver's phone → on the ticket → paid → Active.
  2. Take: Pay · Gift card → scan, type or find by phone → checks real · this salon · active · not locked · balance → use, pay the rest.
  3. Check: client link, or desk scan/type.
  4. Lost: find by phone → Lock → Replace (digital or plastic) → balance moves, logged.

How the client connects

  • Points: automatic by phone. First visit creates the client; online booking with the same phone = same client.
  • Client page: link in every receipt, opens with a one-time text code. Shows points, rewards, history, referral link, gift cards.
  • Gift card sent to their phone: linked automatically.
  • Plastic or forwarded code: client page · Add a gift card · type the code → checked → linked. Or the desk links it at checkout.
  • One card links to one phone; unlinked cards still work by code.

Points, perks, referrals

  • Ticket paid → +1 point per $1 of service (not tips, tax, gift card sales). Refund → taken back.
  • Reward ready → green tag → desk asks → use → −100 points.
  • Perks (birthday, rebook, VIP) are added by the system as their own line; desk can remove; one per visit.
  • Referral code LAN42 + link: friend gets $10 on the first visit; the sharer gets $10 once that visit is paid.

Money rules

  • Gift card sold = money owed, shown separately in Close day, not service sales.
  • Service paid by gift card = a normal sale; tech paid as usual; points earned.
  • Rewards and perks come off the salon's share; techs paid on full price; tips untouched.
  • Owner sees “owed on gift cards” in Numbers. Cash back for small balances follows state law.

Database (Supabase)

Ledgers, not numbers: loyalty_ledger, gift_card_ledger, package_ledger get one insert-only row per change (+52 earn, −100 redeem, +$50 sold, −$50 used). One server function runs when a ticket is paid, all or nothing. Balances are sums read from views (client_points, gift_card_balance) by checkout, receipts, the client page and Numbers. Every row has salon_id + row-level security; devices can't write ledgers; codes are hashed.
