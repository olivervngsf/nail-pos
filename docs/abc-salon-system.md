## Analysis
- **The POS is a $40 commodity.** All three packages have the same POS. They differ only in marketing (posts, SEO, video). The money is in hardware ($1,900–2,500) and agency work ($288–475/mo).
- **Year-1 cost for a salon:**
  - Software + cheapest kit (PAX A920, monthly): $40 + $160 = **$200/mo, ~$2,400 year 1**.
  - PRO-X3: **$3,456 year 1**. PROMAX-5: **$5,700 year 1**.
  - Us: $49/mo + Stripe S710 reader (~$299) on their own iPad/computer = **~$887 year 1**.
- **Card processing:** rate not listed. PAX and Clover terminals are usually tied to one processor, so a salon may be locked to theirs. Ask.
- **After year 1:** "full service for the 1st year" — price for year 2 not listed. Ask.
- **They ship fast:** changelog shows weekly updates in Oct 2026 (staff action log, VIP/Warning/Blacklist clients, hourly commission view, decimal hours, manager ticket permissions). Not a sleepy rival.
- **They have Reserve with Google** (booking from Google Maps). We don't yet.

## What users say (App Store, ABC Go: 3.0 stars, 22 ratings)
- Oct 2026, tech: services and tips "mysteriously disappeared", app shows "Something Went Wrong", a tip for 2 techs went all to one ticket. "We should not have to constantly double-check a system that is supposed to accurately track our work and earnings."
- Oct 2026, owner: can't see appointments remotely, only near the salon computer.
- Nov 2025, owner: wants gift card report (sold, outstanding, redeemed).
- Oct 2026: likes new clock-in status view.

## Watch out: review filtering
- Their check-in "prevents sending SMS to customers who are not satisfied" and "limits bad reviews". Asking only happy clients for reviews ("review gating") is against Google's review rules and may be a risk under the FTC review rule. We should not copy it. Elang: check with the lawyer.

## Where we can beat them
1. **Hardware cost.** ~$299 S710 reader + the salon's own iPad vs $1,900–2,500. Lead the pitch with "year 1: ~$890 vs ~$2,400". (Later, with our phone app, the $59 M2 reader drops this to ~$650.)
2. **Pay techs can trust.** Their #1 complaint is lost tips and split tips going to one tech. Ours: split tip by tech, every ticket change logged, tech sees own earnings any time on their phone.
3. **Works from anywhere.** Owner sees today's sales, calendar and payroll remotely (their app can't).
4. **Gift card report** in V1: sold, outstanding, redeemed.
5. **Honest reviews.** Ask every client the same way; no filtering. Safer and a trust point.
6. **Card fee at Stripe's cost, shown on every ticket**, no terminal lock-in.
7. **Design.** Calmer, simpler screens for senior staff.

## Where they're ahead (match or skip)
- Offline + 5-minute restore, 2-step checkout: **match** (already our core rule).
- Reserve with Google: **consider for V1.1**.
- Marketing agency (posts, SEO, video): **skip building it**. Option later: partner with an agency, or a small "Google profile setup" in a Plus plan.
- Client tags VIP / Warning / Blacklist, staff action log: **cheap to add**; log is already in our plan.

## Should we sell a "basic tier" like theirs?
- Yes, and we already have it: one plan, all features, all staff. Their $40 confirms the price point; ours at $49 ($39 pilot) is fine if we lead on no-hardware cost and tech pay trust.
- Don't copy their tier ladder: their tiers are marketing bundles, not POS levels.
