## Features named
- Service tickets, turn list (chia lượt), payroll (tính lương), client reviews, works offline.
- Go app: owner + staff see reports and payroll on phone; payroll summary; hourly pay.

## Complaints
- App Store review: missing services, tips disappearing, wrong earnings. Trust in pay math = the weak spot.

## What it means for us
- Their "basic" = $40/mo software, all staff. Same shape as our $49 flat plan; confirms ~$40–49 works for Vietnamese owners.
- Our V1 already covers their core (tickets, turns, payroll, offline). Don't copy their tiers; their tiers are hardware bundles.
- Win where they slip: pay math techs can trust (tips + fees on every ticket), clean design, card reader without a $160–475/mo bundle.
