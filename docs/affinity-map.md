# Affinity Map — Nail POS (draft, Nov 15 2026)
Sources: S1 Elang + Laura (via July Google Doc + chat), S2 Oliver's auntie, U1 calendar UI feedback, R1–R10 online reviews/articles (unverified search snippets). Status: clusters agreed at checkpoint 3; not yet on a whiteboard.

## C1 "I need turns to be fair, because turns are my income"
- G1 "A $15 polish and a $95 full set both cost me one turn; every dollar is today's income" — S1
- G2 "On slow days, turn order decides whether I make rent" — S1, R10
- G3 "I lose turns to requests, English speakers or skill routing I can't see" — S1

## C2 "I only trust what everyone can see and nobody can quietly change"
- G4 "I don't trust whoever picks who's next; they can skip me and benefit, so I go quiet or leave" — S1, R7
- G5 "I trust paper and printouts because everyone sees them and no one can quietly change them" — S1, S2, U1

## C3 "I need to see exactly how my pay and tips are worked out"
- G7 "I'm paid in ways I can't check: part check, part cash, commission not hours" — R8, R9
- G8 "Fees come out of my card tips, or tips are held, so I can't count on them" — R10

## C4 "I'm tired of software nickel-and-diming me"
- G10 "I keep getting charged for things I didn't expect" — R1–R3
- G11 "My money is slow unless I pay extra" — R3, R4

## C5 "I need tools that just work for a walk-in salon and low-tech staff"
- G12 "I'm too busy running the floor to set up software; I want a full package that works" — S1
- G13 "My staff won't learn new apps; they live by phone numbers and a PIN" — S1
- G15 "Booking and report tools aren't built for walk-in salons" — R5, R6

## Outliers
- Customer pushed to tip cash (R10) — only customer note.

## Coverage & bias
- C1 rests mostly on S1 (one source). C3, C4 online only, not Vietnamese-specific. G4/G5 lean on generalizations. No first-hand tech/front-desk/customer voices.
